Media Curation
What is media curation — and why does it matter?

Media curation is the art and science of selecting, qualifying, and assembling the precise combination of audiences and inventory to make every advertising impression count. It goes beyond buying media — it's about curating the right context.

1

Audience Intelligence

We analyze behavioral signals, contextual data, and first-party inputs to map out your ideal audience segments — across 300,000+ proprietary taxonomy nodes spanning every vertical.

2

Inventory Qualification

We manually and algorithmically vet publisher inventory against brand safety, viewability, and contextual relevance standards — creating curated deal packages on every major SSP.

3

Deal Packaging & Activation

Curated audiences and premium inventory are packaged into Private Marketplace (PMP) and Curated Deals, ready to activate programmatically on your DSP of choice — no friction, no waste.

4

Measurement & Optimization

Full-funnel reporting covers viewability, engagement, brand safety, and conversion metrics. We continuously optimize deals to improve performance throughout the campaign lifecycle.

Privacy-First Curation
Built for a cookieless world

Third-party cookies are gone. QRate's curation model was designed from the ground up to thrive without them — using contextual signals, first-party data enrichment, and semantic audience modeling to maintain precision at scale.

Contextual Targeting

Without relying on user-level identifiers, QRate uses deep semantic analysis of page content, URL taxonomy, and real-time contextual signals to place your ads in environments that attract your ideal audience — no cookies required.

First-Party Data Activation

Bring your own customer data and we'll amplify it. QRate's curation infrastructure enables secure first-party audience onboarding, privacy-compliant matching, and extension across premium publisher environments.

Brand Safety by Default

Every QRate deal includes multi-layer brand safety: pre-bid contextual filtering, publisher-level whitelisting, and post-bid verification against IAS and DoubleVerify standards. Brand safety is never an add-on — it's built in.

About QRate
Founded by digital media pioneers.
Built for what's next.

QRate was created to address the growing complexity of the digital advertising ecosystem. As fragmentation increased and signal quality deteriorated, the industry needed a smarter approach — not more targeting options, but better curation.

Our founding team brings together decades of experience spanning programmatic trading, data science, publisher monetization, and media strategy. We built QRate because we believed — and still believe — that curation is the future of digital media quality.

Today, QRate operates as a trusted curation partner for leading advertisers and publishers across Europe and beyond, delivering precision media strategies that connect brands with the right audiences at the right time.
